# Daily Spend

A simple expense tracker that runs in your browser and works offline after first load.

## Open the app

https://mhatrechirag333-source.github.io/daily-spend/

## Records and privacy

Your expense records are saved in the browser's local storage on the device where you enter them. They remain after you close and reopen the app in the same browser, but they do not sync to other devices. Use **Export** in the app to download a CSV backup.
